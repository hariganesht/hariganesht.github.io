There are two reasons I write here: I believe the manner in which knowledge is presented in academia should be far more accessible (justified nicely in this Distill post by Chris Olah and Shan Carter), and that there is some merit in keeping an iterative record of my learning.

There is also an excellent post by Rachel Thomas on why this might be a useful thing to do in academia.
